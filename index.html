🕵️ The Impostor Battle

Two teams of 15. Four impostors hide in each team. Three rounds. English only.

TOP SECRET

Rules of the battle

1

Get your card

Each team gets its own secret word from the round's theme. 11 players see the word. The 4 impostors only see "???".

2

Talk in English

Ask and answer questions with your team. Never say the secret word! Impostors must sound like they know it.

3

Race to finish

Your team finishes the round in one of two ways, and the first team to finish wins.

A. The impostors guess the secret word.
B. The crew finds all 4 impostors.

4

Limits and penalties

8 minutes per round. Only the captain accuses. A wrong accusation means 15 seconds of silence. A wrong word guess reveals that impostor.

Points

First team to finish: +3. The other team: +1 for each impostor it found. Spot an impostor by vague answers ("It's good"), repeated words, long pauses and Spanish.

CASE FILE 01

Round 1: Food 🍕

Is your teammate really eating the same thing as you?

Questions to ask

  • Is it sweet, salty, sour or spicy?
  • Do you eat it hot or cold?
  • Is it for breakfast, lunch or dinner?
  • What color is it?
  • Do you eat it with your hands?
  • Where can you buy it?

Words to listen for

crunchyjuicycreamyfriedbakedfreshsaucesnackdeliciousdessert

Real players give details. Impostors use only general words like "good" or "nice".

CASE FILE 02

Round 2: Animals 🐾

Who knows this animal, and who is only guessing?

Questions to ask

  • Where does it live?
  • How many legs does it have?
  • Is it big or small?
  • What does it eat?
  • Can it fly, swim or run fast?
  • Would you keep it as a pet?

Words to listen for

wingsfurtailclawswildfarmoceanforestpredatorpet

Listen for "because". Real players explain their answers. Impostors copy the last person.

🔒 Secret word

sharkdolphinhorsedonkey

CASE FILE 03

Round 3: Objects 🎒

Last round. Every detail counts.

Questions to ask

  • Where do you usually find it?
  • What is it made of?
  • Do you use it every day?
  • Is it bigger than a backpack?
  • Does it need electricity or batteries?
  • Who uses it the most?

Words to listen for

plasticmetalbatteryscreenhandleheavypocketkitchenschooluseful

Ask follow-up questions: "Why do you say that?" Impostors struggle with the second question.

glassesbinocularstablettelevision
